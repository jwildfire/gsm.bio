# Synthetic biomarker study: outcomes

One row per participant and endpoint of the made-up study in
[Synthetic_Results](https://jwildfire.github.io/gsm.bio/reference/Synthetic_Results.md),
with a time and a censor flag. There is one endpoint, event-free
survival in months.

## Usage

``` r
Synthetic_Outcomes
```

## Format

A data frame with 200 rows and 5 columns. The names are ADaM's
time-to-event names.

- USUBJID:

  `character` Participant identifier, `BIO-001` to `BIO-200`.

- PARAMCD:

  `character` Endpoint code, `EFS`.

- PARAM:

  `character` Endpoint name, `Event-free survival (months)`.

- AVAL:

  `numeric` Time to the event or to censoring, in months, to two decimal
  places.

- CNSR:

  `integer` Censor flag, ADaM's way round: 0 for an event, 1 for a
  censored time.

## Source

Generated by `data-raw/synthetic-study.R`. The same table is in the
package as
`system.file("extdata", "synthetic_outcomes.csv", package = "gsm.bio")`.

## Details

Everything in it is synthetic: the times are random draws, not
observations.

## Planted effect

Participants are split at the median of their Baseline CRP in
[Synthetic_Results](https://jwildfire.github.io/gsm.bio/reference/Synthetic_Results.md),
100 above it and 100 below, with no value on the median. Event times are
exponential, and the hazard in the high group is the hazard in the low
group multiplied by a constant: a hazard ratio of 2.5, high against low.
CRP has no other relationship with the outcome, and no other biomarker
has any.

Censoring is independent of the event: a participant drops out at an
exponential time, and follow-up ends at 24 months. The true hazard ratio
is a parameter of the generating model, held in
[Synthetic_Truth](https://jwildfire.github.io/gsm.bio/reference/Synthetic_Truth.md).
