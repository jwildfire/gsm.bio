# Synthetic biomarker study: participants

One row per participant of the made-up study in
[Synthetic_Results](https://jwildfire.github.io/gsm.bio/reference/Synthetic_Results.md):
the treatment arm, and the participant-level variables a chart can
group, panel or filter by.

## Usage

``` r
Synthetic_Participants
```

## Format

A data frame with 200 rows and 6 columns. The names follow ADaM's
subject-level dataset where it has one.

- USUBJID:

  `character` Participant identifier, `BIO-001` to `BIO-200`.

- ARM:

  `character` Treatment arm, `Placebo` or `Treatment`, 100 participants
  each.

- SEX:

  `character` `F` or `M`.

- AGE:

  `integer` Age in years, 18 to 85.

- BMIBL:

  `numeric` Body mass index at baseline in kg/m2, to one decimal place:
  the continuous baseline characteristic.

- RESPONSE:

  `character` `Responder` or `Non-responder`.

## Source

Generated by `data-raw/synthetic-study.R`. The same table is in the
package as
`system.file("extdata", "synthetic_participants.csv", package = "gsm.bio")`.

## Details

Everything in it is synthetic. Arm is the grouping behind the planted
difference in IL-6 (see
[Synthetic_Results](https://jwildfire.github.io/gsm.bio/reference/Synthetic_Results.md)).
The other columns are drawn independently of each other, of arm, of
every biomarker and of the outcome, so any association a test finds with
them is chance.
